Websites That Give Your Brand a Stronger Online Presence

I build modern landing pages and functional websites that help brands showcase what they do, connect with customers, and grow their online presence.

MOIZ.UX is the personal web development portfolio and service business of Moiz. I build modern landing pages, business websites, and functional web applications for small businesses, startups, creators, and independent brands.

Services

Landing Pages: Focused single-page experiences for brands, products, and campaigns. Includes hero sections, features, FAQs, and responsive layouts.

Business & Portfolio Websites: Multi-page brand homes for businesses and creators. Includes about pages, service pages, project showcases, and contact forms.

Functional Websites: Beyond static content. Includes contact forms, database-backed content, dynamic listings, and third-party integrations.

Projects

Pixel Quest: A gamified habit tracker built to turn daily routines into an RPG-style adventure with XP, levels, and streaks.

Process

1. Discovery: Understand the business, audience, and requirements.

2. Planning: Agree on scope, content, timeline, and budget.

3. Development: Build the agreed website and functionality.

4. Review: Test the website and collect feedback.

5. Launch: Deploy the approved website and hand over.
