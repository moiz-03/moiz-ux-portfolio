# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## MOIZ.UX — Personal Website & Web Development Portfolio

* **Document version:** 1.0
* **Product type:** Personal portfolio and service-business website
* **Brand:** MOIZ.UX
* **Primary acquisition channel:** Instagram (@moiz.ux)
* **Primary objective:** Generate qualified website-development enquiries from brands and businesses.

---

## 1. Product Overview

### 1.1 Product Vision
MOIZ.UX is a personal web-development brand that helps businesses, startups, creators, and independent brands establish a professional online presence.

The website will serve as the brand's official digital home, showcasing completed projects, explaining services, establishing credibility, and converting interested visitors into potential clients.

The website must communicate that MOIZ.UX builds functional, responsive websites—not simply visual designs or static mockups.

### 1.2 Problem Statement
Many small businesses and emerging brands lack a dedicated website that presents their identity professionally, explains their offerings, and makes it easy for customers to contact them.

Potential clients also need a straightforward way to evaluate a developer's capabilities before discussing a project.

MOIZ.UX needs a professional portfolio website that demonstrates its work, communicates its services, builds trust, and provides a direct path from discovering the brand on Instagram to making a project enquiry.

### 1.3 Product Goals
1. Establish a credible and professional online identity for MOIZ.UX.
2. Showcase website-development capabilities through real projects and working demonstrations.
3. Explain available services in language that nontechnical clients understand.
4. Generate relevant enquiries from potential clients.
5. Provide a central link for the Instagram bio.
6. Create a scalable foundation for future projects, services, and case studies.

### 1.4 Success Criteria
The website should enable a first-time visitor to:
* Understand what MOIZ.UX does within a few seconds.
* View projects and access live demonstrations.
* Understand which services are available.
* Find a clear way to request a website.
* Use the website comfortably on mobile devices.
* Contact the developer without creating an account.

## 5. Website Structure and Information Architecture

The initial website will be a streamlined portfolio and service landing page.

### 5.1 Main Navigation
* Home
* Services
* Projects
* About
* Contact

Navigation should scroll to the appropriate section or open the corresponding page. On mobile, use a compact navigation menu that is accessible by keyboard and touch.

### 5.2 Page Sections

#### Section A: Hero
* **Purpose:** Communicate the service and value proposition immediately.
* **Suggested headline:** `"Websites That Give Your Brand a Stronger Online Presence."`
* **Suggested supporting text:** `"I build modern landing pages and functional websites that help brands showcase what they do, connect with customers, and grow their online presence."`
* **Primary CTA:** `Let's Build Your Website`
* **Secondary CTA:** `Explore My Work`
* **Requirements:** The hero must include a strong website preview, project visual, or other relevant demonstration of actual work. Avoid invented client logos, fake testimonials, and unsupported business results.

#### Section B: Selected Projects
* **Purpose:** Demonstrate capability through visible work.
* **Project Card Elements:** Project title, Short description, Project category, Technologies used (when relevant), Project thumbnail or screenshot, Live demo link (when available), Case study link (when available).
* **Guidelines:** Project cards should prioritize actual working websites over abstract descriptions. If a project is a personal concept or demonstration, label it clearly as a concept project. If no client projects exist yet, display personal projects honestly rather than inventing client work.

#### Section C: Services
* **Purpose:** Help visitors identify the service they need.
* **Service Cards:** 1. Landing Pages, 2. Business & Portfolio Websites, 3. Functional Websites.
* **Card Content:** Who the service is for, What the website can include, What problem it helps solve, How to enquire about the service.
* **Guidelines:** Avoid fixed pricing until the developer has established reliable package scopes and pricing.

#### Section D: Process
* **Purpose:** Explain how a project moves from initial conversation to delivery.
* **Steps:**
  1. **Discovery:** Understand the business, audience, objectives, and requirements.
  2. **Planning:** Agree on scope, content, features, timeline, and budget.
  3. **Development:** Build the agreed website and functionality.
  4. **Review:** Test the website and collect agreed feedback.
  5. **Launch:** Deploy the approved website and provide the agreed handover.
* **Guidelines:** Set realistic expectations about revisions, client-provided content, delivery, and post-launch support.

#### Section E: About
* **Purpose:** Establish a personal connection and professional credibility.
* **Include:** Name or professional identity, A concise introduction, Focus on website development, Technologies and tools genuinely used, A brief statement about the approach to projects, A professional photo or suitable personal brand visual (if available).
* **Guidelines:** Sound authentic and avoid unsupported claims about years of experience, client numbers, or expertise.

#### Section F: Why Work With Me
* **Purpose:** Communicate the practical benefits of the service.
* **Potential points:** Direct communication with the developer, Clear project scope, Responsive website implementation, Focus on functionality and maintainability, Project-specific solutions, Transparent discussion of requirements and limitations.
* **Guidelines:** Only make promises that can be consistently fulfilled.

#### Section G: Contact and Final CTA
* **Purpose:** Convert interested visitors into project enquiries.
* **Suggested heading:** `"Have a Website Idea? Let's Talk."`
* **Supporting text:** `"Tell me about your brand, what you want to build, and what you need your website to accomplish."`
* **Provide:** WhatsApp contact link, Professional email address, Instagram profile link, Project enquiry form.
* **Requirements:** The WhatsApp link and email address must be configurable rather than hardcoded throughout the application.

#### Section H: Footer
* **Include:** MOIZ.UX brand identity, Short service description, Navigation links, Instagram link, Contact details, Copyright notice, Privacy policy link if data is collected through the website.

## 6. Functional Requirements

* **FR-01: Responsive Website:** The website must work across mobile phones, tablets, laptops, and desktop displays.
* **FR-02: Project Showcase:** Visitors must be able to browse the available projects with live links and details.
* **FR-03: Service Information:** Visitors must be able to understand services and deliverables without technical jargon.
* **FR-04: Contact Actions:** Actionable WhatsApp, Email, and Instagram direct connectivity.
* **FR-05: Project Enquiry Form:** Structured contact collection (Name, Email, Website Type, Description, Budget, Timeline) with input validation.
* **FR-06: Project Data Management:** Maintainable JSON/static file structure for content rendering without requiring database overhead for MVP.
* **FR-07: Instagram Integration:** Seamless cross-channel bio link targeting and profile transition.
* **FR-08: SEO and Sharing:** Complete meta metadata, OpenGraph tags, semantic HTML, and structured data execution.

---

## 7. Non-Functional Requirements

### 7.1 Performance
* Optimize image sizes and WebP/AVIF formats.
* Avoid unnecessary JavaScript and large dependencies.
* Minimize layout shifts (CLS < 0.1).
* Achieve sub-1.2s LCP on mobile 4G network profiles.

### 7.2 Accessibility
* Semantic HTML5 markup.
* WCAG 2.1 Level AA contrast ratio adherence (4.5:1 for normal text).
* Keyboard navigable components with visible focus rings.

### 7.3 Security
* Client and server-side input sanitization.
* CSP (Content Security Policy) and CORS restrictions.
* Honeypot and Rate Limiting on contact form endpoints.

### 7.4 Maintainability
* Strict separation of content data arrays and UI presentation components.
* Standardized design tokens.

---

## 8. Recommended Technical Approach
* **Frontend:** React + Vite / Next.js static output utilizing Tailwind CSS for styling.
* **Hosting:** Vercel or Cloudflare Pages with Global CDN distribution.
* **Forms:** Serverless API function coupled with Resend or Formspree for direct email dispatch without database dependencies.

---

## 9. Design Requirements
* **Visual Direction:** Modern dark-theme aesthetic featuring deep navy/slate background (`#0B0F17`), neon electric cyan accents (`#00F0FF`), crisp white headers (`#F8FAFC`), and subtle border treatments.
* **Mobile-First:** Tailored touch interfaces and optimized viewport vertical layout hierarchy.
