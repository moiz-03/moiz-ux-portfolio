Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

1. Summary

This site collects information only through the contact form. No tracking cookies are used. No analytics profiles are built. Your data is used solely to respond to your enquiry.

2. Information I Collect

When you submit the contact form, I collect:

  • Name — to address you personally
  • Email address — to reply to your enquiry
  • Business name (optional) — to understand your context
  • Preferred contact method — to reach you the way you prefer
  • Website type — to understand what you need
  • Project description — to evaluate your requirements
  • Budget range (optional) — to gauge project scope fit
  • Desired timeline (optional) — to understand urgency
  • Submission timestamp — for reference and follow-up

3. How Information Is Used

Contact form submissions are used exclusively to:

  • Reply to your enquiry
  • Discuss potential project scope
  • Send follow-up communication about your project

Your information will never be sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. How Information Is Processed

To deliver services reliably, your submission passes through:

  • Vercel — hosting provider (edge CDN). Serves this site and processes form submissions. Vercel Privacy Policy
  • Resend — transactional email service. Delivers your enquiry to my inbox. Resend Privacy Policy
  • Gmail (Google) — where I receive and read enquiries. Google Privacy Policy

5. Cookies and Tracking

This website does not use cookies, tracking pixels, analytics scripts, or third-party trackers. No profile of your browsing behavior is created. There is no need for a cookie consent banner because no cookies are set.

6. Data Retention

Enquiries are retained in my email inbox for as long as necessary to discuss the project. If no project proceeds, enquiries are deleted after 12 months. You may request earlier deletion at any time.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location (particularly EU/UK under GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the data I hold about you
  • Rectification — request corrections to inaccurate data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restriction — request that I limit how your data is used
  • Objection — object to processing your data
  • Portability — request a machine-readable export of your data
  • Complaint — lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email code.moiz2003@gmail.com. I will respond within 30 days.

8. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the EU/UK, the legal basis for processing your enquiry data is:

  • Legitimate interest — you contacted me about potential services; responding is the reasonable expectation
  • Contract pre-negotiation — assessing whether we want to enter into a service agreement

9. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS with HSTS enforced. The contact form is rate-limited to prevent abuse. Only I have access to the inbox where enquiries land. I take reasonable technical measures to protect your data but cannot guarantee absolute security of any internet transmission.

10. Children

This site is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has submitted data, email me and I will delete it.

11. International Transfers

Vercel, Resend, and Google are based in the United States. If you are in the EU/UK, submitting the contact form involves transferring your data to the United States. These providers maintain appropriate safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses) for such transfers.

12. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated periodically. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent version. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

13. Contact
